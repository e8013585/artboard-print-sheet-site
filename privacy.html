Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Artboard Print Sheet ("the extension") is a free Adobe Illustrator extension published by Artboard Print Sheet.

The extension does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

  • No network access. The extension makes no internet connections: no analytics, tracking, advertising, crash reporting or update checks.
  • Your documents stay on your computer. The extension reads the artboards and artwork of the open Illustrator document, and it creates new documents on your computer. It does not upload or copy them anywhere else.
  • Settings are stored locally only. Your panel settings (units, page size, margins, etc.) are kept on your computer:
    • The panel keeps them in its local browser storage.
    • The standalone script keeps them in a small file, ArtboardPrintSheet/settings.json, inside your user application-data folder.
    • Deleting that storage or file resets the settings.
  • No accounts. The extension has no sign-in and no user accounts.

If you install the extension through Adobe Exchange or the Adobe Creative Cloud desktop app, Adobe's own privacy policy covers that installation process: https://www.adobe.com/privacy.html
